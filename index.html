MyHoleLife privacy policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026 · App: MyHoleLife (life.myhole.app) · Publisher: GameSlop · Contact: notabotherin@gmail.com

What the app stores

MyHoleLife is a single-player top-down den builder and extract dungeon RPG. Progress (den/hole layout, inventory, cosmetics, settings, tutorial flags) is saved on the device. v1 does not require an account and does not upload your den or dungeon runs to a studio server.

Purchases

Google Play processes in-app purchases. We receive a purchase token and product id to unlock what you bought. We do not receive your full payment card number. Purchases can be restored from your Google account where Play supports that.

Optional online features

Sign-in and online scoreboards are not required. If a later build adds Play Games Services, that provider's policy applies and this page will be updated first.

What we do not collect by default

  • Precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone for gameplay
  • Advertising ID (no ads in shipped builds)
  • Analytics / Crashlytics until this policy and Play Data safety are updated

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Uninstalling deletes the on-device save. Play purchases remain on your Google account.

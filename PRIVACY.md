# MyHoleLife privacy policy

**Effective date:** 29 September 2026  
**App:** MyHoleLife (`life.myhole.app`)  
**Publisher:** GameSlop  
**Contact:** notabotherin@gmail.com

## What the app stores

MyHoleLife is a single-player top-down den builder and extract dungeon RPG. Progress (den/hole layout, inventory, cosmetics owned, settings, tutorial flags) is saved **on the device** using Android app preferences / local storage.

v1 does **not** require an account and does **not** upload your den or dungeon runs to a studio server.

## Purchases

If you buy in-app products on Google Play (for example Extra Floor, Extra Space, cosmetics, or shard packs), Google processes the payment. We receive a purchase token and product id so we can unlock what you paid for. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Purchases can be restored from your Google account after reinstall where Google Play supports that for the product type.

## Optional online features

Sign-in and online scoreboards are **not** required to play. If a later build adds Google Play Games Services (sign-in, achievements, or leaderboards), that provider's privacy policy applies to the login and scores you choose to submit. This policy will be updated before those features ship.

## What we do not collect by default

- Precise location
- Contacts
- Photos or microphone for gameplay
- Advertising ID (the shipped builds do not show ads)
- Analytics / Crashlytics SDKs (none included until this policy and Play Data safety are updated)

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Do not enable optional sign-in or online scores for that age group without a separate COPPA review.

## Your choices

Uninstalling the app deletes the on-device save. Google Play purchases remain on your Google account and can be restored when the product type allows.

## Changes

If we change what the app stores or sends, we will update this page and the Play Console Data safety form to match.
